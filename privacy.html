Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Youtube Engine is a personal tool operated by one individual for their own YouTube channel. It is not available to other users.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, it accesses the owner's own channel: uploading videos, reading video and channel analytics, and reading and replying to comments on the owner's videos.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to manage the owner's channel. Analytics and comment data are stored privately for up to 30 days and then refreshed or deleted, as required by the YouTube API Services policies. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
